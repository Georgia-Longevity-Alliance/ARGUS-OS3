## 2. The three kill-shots — all real, all integrated (v8.2)

### Kill-shot 1: Ciliary-proliferation paradox → SOLVED (§7.1)
Fibroblasts resorb the primary cilium at G1/S — one cannot measure EdU+ (proliferation) and cilium signaling (H3/H8) in the same cell at the same time.

**Resolution:** CRCS measured in **two defined regimes**: (a) proliferative mode (EdU+, CFU, γH2AX, SA-β-gal, TMRM, Horvath on dividing clones); (b) **contact-inhibition/quiescence G0** (cilia present: length, Hedgehog/Wnt targets, ciliary proteome). Cilia-dependent hypotheses (H3, H8) evaluated in G0 only; the two domains reported separately, never pooled across incompatible phases.

### Kill-shot 2: Temporal dissonance OSK vs. Reset → SOLVED (Arm F, §6.1)
OSK takes 10–14 days; reset takes 3–5 days. In simultaneous arm D, OSK-driven cytoplasmic remodeling could pre-clean the cytoplasm before de novo assembly — making H3 (independence) untestable.

**Resolution:** **sixth arm F — Reset → washout → 10 passages → OSK**: if reset benefits persist through washout and cytoplasmic re-aging, the structural reset has long-term memory (CEDAR prediction); if they decay, the "reset" was OSK/cytoplasm-dependent (confounder). Arm F is the temporal control that makes H3 interpretable.

### Kill-shot 3: AID2 + cytostatic-window trap → SOLVED (§6.2b, dual synchronization)
AID2 degradation is not instantaneous (24–48 h, dilutive); cells already in G2 at auxin addition would enter mitosis with partially degraded centrioles → multipolar spindles → micronuclei → cGAS–STING. CDK4/6i does not stop G2 cells.

**Resolution:** **dual synchronization**: (1) aphidicolin (S-phase block) synchronizes the population; (2) washout → auxin + RO-3306 (CDK1i, strict G2 block) for 12 h — DNA replication completes, mitosis impossible until SAS-6 degradation is verified >90% by western blot; only then release into the cytostatic window. "Eliminate with the entire population pinned in G2."
